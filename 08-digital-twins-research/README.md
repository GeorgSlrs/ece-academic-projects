# Digital Twins Research

An individual literature-based coursework paper from 2021–22 examining what a digital twin represents and how data from a physical system can support its digital counterpart. The paper connects the concept to IoT sensing, artificial intelligence and machine learning, then discusses applications in smart cities, healthcare and industrial production.

## Scope

- Definitions and the relationship between the physical system, sensors and digital model.
- The roles of IoT data, simulation and AI/ML in monitoring and decision support.
- Predictive maintenance, production quality and possible healthcare/city applications.
- Benefits and practical concerns, including complexity, cost and data security.

## Read the paper

[DIGITAL TWINS.docx](DIGITAL%20TWINS.docx) contains the Greek-language paper, illustrations and bibliography. Open it with a DOCX-compatible document viewer.

This is a research and science-writing artifact, with no accompanying digital-twin software implementation or experimental dataset. Its examples and outlook reflect the sources available when the coursework was written.
