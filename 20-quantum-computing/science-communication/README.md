## Topic
The piece uses the Game of Life / cellular-automaton idea as an accessible route into quantum-computing concepts. It is included here as communication/outreach work rather than as a claim of having developed the underlying research models.

## Related background
Foundational research on quantum versions of the Game of Life / quantum cellular automata includes work by Bleh, Calarco & Montangero (2010) and Arrighi & Grattage (2010). This folder links the independent communication artifact to the broader quantum-computing coursework already stored one directory above.
