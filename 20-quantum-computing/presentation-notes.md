# Quantum Computing & Quantum Gates - Presentation Notes

Sanitized text export from the original team presentation. Teammate names and student identifiers have been omitted.

## Topics covered

### Pauli gates
The presentation introduced the Pauli I, X, Y and Z single-qubit gates. X was related to the classical NOT operation, while Y and Z were discussed in terms of state changes and phase/rotation effects.

### CNOT
The controlled-NOT gate was presented as a two-qubit gate with a control and target qubit. The target changes when the control is in state |1>, while the control remains unchanged. The presentation also discussed reversibility and the role of CNOT in quantum logic.

### Controlled phase and Toffoli / CCNOT
The team covered controlled phase-shift gates and the three-qubit Toffoli gate, describing the two control qubits and target behaviour.

### Identity, phase and Hadamard gates
Additional introductory quantum gates included identity/phase-shift operations and the Hadamard gate.

### Quantum errors and decoherence
The presentation discussed two broad error ideas: bit-flip-type errors and decoherence caused by unwanted interaction with the environment. It introduced the motivation for quantum error-correction schemes and the difficulty of protecting quantum information without directly measuring the encoded state.

### Introductory quantum algorithms
The presentation concluded with short introductions to:
- Shor's integer-factorization algorithm
- Deutsch's problem
- Grover search
- quantum teleportation concepts
