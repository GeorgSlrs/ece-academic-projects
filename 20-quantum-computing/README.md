# Quantum Computing & Quantum Gates

Introductory ECE coursework and related science-communication material exploring how quantum information is represented and manipulated. The team presentation uses gate diagrams, matrices and examples to introduce quantum logic, while a separate communication piece uses the Game of Life as an accessible entry into quantum-computing ideas.

## Topics

- Single-qubit gates: Pauli, identity, phase and Hadamard operations.
- Controlled gates: CNOT, controlled phase and Toffoli/CCNOT.
- Bit-flip errors, environmental decoherence and the motivation for error correction.
- Introductions to Deutsch's problem, Shor's algorithm, Grover search and quantum teleportation.
