# Capacitive Sensor & Accelerometer Design

Team coursework for Electrical Measurement Devices & Techniques (2023–24), connecting a capacitive measurement circuit to a mechanical accelerometer model. The work follows the signal from displacement-dependent capacitance through electrical measurement and timing/counting concepts to a digital indication.

## Design study

- **Capacitive sensing:** sensor sensitivity, RC measurement and loading errors introduced by cables and oscilloscope capacitance.
- **Signal conditioning:** buffer/op-amp concepts and practical current-source considerations.
- **Timing and digitization:** phase-to-time conversion, an SR flip-flop and clock-pulse counting, including a resolution calculation for the output range.
- **Accelerometer dynamics:** a mass-spring-damper model, second-order transfer functions, damping/bandwidth choices and MATLAB response studies.
